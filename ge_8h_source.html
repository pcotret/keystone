15/*
16ge means group element.
17
18Here the group is the set of pairs (x,y) of field elements (see fe.h)
19satisfying -x^2 + y^2 = 1 + d x^2y^2
20where d = -121665/121666.
21
22Representations:
23 ge_p2 (projective): (X:Y:Z) satisfying x=X/Z, y=Y/Z
24 ge_p3 (extended): (X:Y:Z:T) satisfying x=X/Z, y=Y/Z, XY=ZT
25 ge_p1p1 (completed): ((X:Z),(Y:T)) satisfying x=X/Z, y=Y/T
26 ge_precomp (Duif): (y+x,y-x,2dxy)
27*/
